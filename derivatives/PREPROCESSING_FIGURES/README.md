## Points worth making at the panel

- **165 / 173** eligible acquisitions preprocessed, from **127 distinct
  subjects**. Both exclusions and all 8 failures are itemised, not rounded away.
- **All 8 failures share one cause**: TR ≈ 6.02 s gives a Nyquist of 0.083 Hz,
  below the 0.10 Hz filter cutoff. All 8 are from site 002. The pipeline
  refused to band-pass them rather than silently narrowing the band.
- **No T1w image exists for any of the 131 subjects**, so registration is a
  direct EPI → MNI SyN warp. This is why CNR is reported as N/A everywhere —
  it is not computable, not missing. `fig4` is the evidence the warp worked.
- **27 nuisance regressors in every single acquisition** — intercept,
  Friston-24, white matter, global signal. CSF failed its reliability gate in
  all 165, so it was dropped rather than forced in. The count is identical
  across the dataset, so no acquisition got a different model.
- **Median in-band power fraction is 93.0 %, minimum 82.3 %** across all 165 —
  the 0.01–0.10 Hz band-pass demonstrably did what it claims.
- **High-motion acquisitions were flagged, not deleted**: 134 above 0.2 mm,
  28 above 0.5 mm. Motion enters the modelling stage as a covariate.
- One CN acquisition (`sub-018S4313/ses-01/run-01`) is an artifact: tSNR
  322,315 against a cohort median of 128. It is excluded from the summary
  statistics in `fig2`/`fig3`/`fig5` and labelled as such, not quietly removed.
