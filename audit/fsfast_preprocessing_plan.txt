--- CRITICAL FS-FAST ANALYSIS ---
Can resting-state preprocessing be performed with FS-FAST WITHOUT a T1w image?
Answer: STRICTLY NO for a standard FS-FAST workflow.
Reasoning:
FS-FAST (FreeSurfer Functional Analysis Stream) is explicitly designed to integrate functional MRI with FreeSurfer's structural cortical surface models (recon-all). Core commands like 'preproc-sess' heavily depend on 'bbregister' (boundary-based registration), which requires the cortical ribbon generated by 'recon-all'. Without T1w images, 'recon-all' cannot run, and FS-FAST cannot perform anatomical registration, surface projection, or extract WM/CSF nuisance regressors for resting-state connectivity.
